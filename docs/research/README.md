# Research Directory
Central archive for strategy research plans, campaigns, and hypothesis ledgers.

## 1. Purpose
Tracks quantitative hypotheses, inventory replays, backtest plans, and active research questions.

## 2. What Belongs Here
* Hypotheses ledger reconstruction files and research plans.
* Active questions registries.
* Campaign documents.

## 3. What Does NOT Belong Here
* Live trading code configurations.
* VCS build/commit reports.
* Hard validation logs.

## 4. Read-First Files
* Check [docs/research/ACTIVE_QUESTION.md](file:///c:/quant/Polymarket-Adaptive-Quant-Engine-v.1/docs/research/ACTIVE_QUESTION.md) to locate the current research focus.
* Check [docs/research/DECISION_REGISTRY.json](file:///c:/quant/Polymarket-Adaptive-Quant-Engine-v.1/docs/research/DECISION_REGISTRY.json) for historical findings.

## 5. Historical Files Policy
Research campaigns are locked once their active questions are resolved. Findings must not be retroactively modified without creating a new follow-up hypothesis.

## 6. Active/Current Files Policy
Only the current campaign files and the active question ledger are updated dynamically during research runs.

## 7. Notes for AI Agents
> [!CAUTION]
> Hard rule: Never open a new research frontier or campaign unless the current active question is explicitly closed.
