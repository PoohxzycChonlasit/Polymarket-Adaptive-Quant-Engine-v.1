→ Result: Win rate 38%, Sharpe -5.06, Max DD 43.6% — FAILING

**New strategy (Apr 19):**
```
momentum_pct = (candle_open - close_3_candles_ago) / close_3_candles_ago * 100

if momentum_pct > +0.40%  → Bet NO   (mean reversion down)
```
