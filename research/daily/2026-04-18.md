## 5. Strategy Architecture — Polymarket 5-Minute

Current strategy uses 4 signals:

```
Signal 1: Window Delta (5-7x weight) — oracle price vs PTB at window open
Signal 2: EMA 9/21 crossover — trend filter
Signal 3: RSI 35–78 range filter — avoid overbought/oversold entries
Signal 4: Volume ratio ≥ 0.4x 20-bar mean — liquidity confirmation
```

**Key finding:** The strategy currently skips 8,752 out of 8,886 windows (98.5% skip rate). On the 134 trades that get through, win rate is only 38% — well below breakeven on a 50/50 binary market with 2% fees.

---

## 6. Root Cause Analysis — Why Strategy Is Failing

1. **EMA filter is wrong for Polymarket:** On 5-min BTC Up/Down, EMA trend logic from crypto scalping does NOT apply. The market already starts at ~50/50 — EMA being "below" does not predict whether next 5-min candle goes up.
2. **Window Delta signal is weak in its current form:** A small +0.17% delta does not reliably predict the direction of the next 5-min window. Need to test larger delta thresholds or momentum continuation.
3. **Fees are brutal at micro bankroll:** With $8.89 and $0.10 minimum bets, fees eat ~20% per trade at these sizes. Breakeven win rate is ~55%+ (not 50%) because of the 2% fee.
4. **RSI filter over-restricts entries:** Skipping when RSI < 35 or > 78 cuts too many potential trades.
