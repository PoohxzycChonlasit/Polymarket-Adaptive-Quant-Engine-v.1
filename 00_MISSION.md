# 00 Mission

## Primary Mission

Build a profitable Polymarket Crypto 5-Minute Quant Profit Engine.

The current target is paper profit first. Micro live is allowed only after paper evidence proves positive expectancy, controlled drawdown, market-realistic execution, and operator-visible safety.

## Current Phase

Phase: Profit Engine.

This repository is not primarily a research platform, framework platform, or architecture platform. Research, architecture, replay, and tooling are useful only when they directly improve the probability of profitable, market-realistic paper trading.

## Market Focus

Current market family:

- Polymarket Crypto 5-Minute Up/Down markets.
- Current assets: BTC and ETH unless explicitly expanded in a future approved task.
- Paper mode first.
- Live mode only after explicit operator approval and readiness evidence.

## Primary KPI

The engine must optimize toward:

- Positive expectancy.
- Controlled drawdown.
- Growing equity curve.
- Entry throughput sufficient for learning and eventual profit.
- Market-realistic execution.
- Market-time-accurate logs.
- 24/7 paper stability.
- Micro live only after paper evidence.

## Profit Filter

Every task must improve at least one of:

- Profitability.
- Expected value.
- Entry throughput.
- Risk or drawdown.
- Execution realism.
- Market reality.
- Position sizing reality.
- Quant math.
- Operator visibility.
- Runtime safety.

If a task does not improve at least one item, route it to `06_BACKLOG.md`.

## Non-Goals Right Now

- Do not create a large research lab.
- Do not create a new strategy framework unless it directly supports profit evidence.
- Do not add markets before BTC/ETH 5M profitability evidence.
- Do not tune thresholds from weak samples.
- Do not touch live trading without explicit operator approval.
- Do not claim profitability without paper evidence.

## Decision Rule

When two paths compete, choose the path that most directly improves paper profitability evidence while preserving safety.
