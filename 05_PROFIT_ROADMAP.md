# 05 Profit Roadmap

## Roadmap Principle

The roadmap is ordered by the shortest path to trustworthy paper profitability evidence.

## Phase 0 - Governance Reset

Status: current.

Goal:

- Make Profit Engine the mission.
- Freeze non-profit framework drift.
- Define modularity, loop engineering, dashboard, and time-truth standards.

Exit:

- Core docs updated.
- Backlog frozen.
- Next runtime task selected by Profit Filter.

## Phase 1 - Truthful Runtime Scoreboard

Goal:

- Show operator the current bottleneck every cycle/session.
- Track trade funnel from decisions to closes and PnL.
- Separate protective gates from over-conservative gates.

Evidence:

- Dashboard/status exposes PnL, equity, entry funnel, blockers, freshness, and sizing realism warnings.

## Phase 2 - Entry Throughput and Market Realism

Goal:

- Determine whether zero-entry periods are correct protection or an over-conservative system design.
- Validate tick size, min order, fee, spread, liquidity, and fillability assumptions.

Evidence:

- Near-miss examples with EV, confidence, spread, depth, seconds to expiry, and MFE/MAE where available.

## Phase 3 - Paper Profit Loop

Goal:

- Produce enough market-realistic paper entries and exits to measure expectancy.

Evidence:

- Positive expectancy or documented bottleneck.
- Cash, exposure, PnL, and trade journal reconcile.
- Timing truth complete enough for replay.

## Phase 4 - Micro Live Readiness

Goal:

- Only after paper profit evidence, validate tiny controlled live execution.

Blocked until:

- Live allowance and order/fill/cancel/reconciliation are proven.
- Operator explicitly approves.

## Frozen Paths

These are deferred unless they pass the Profit Filter:

- Large shadow framework expansion.
- New markets.
- Strategy rewrites.
- Inventory ladder runtime.
- Live pilot.
